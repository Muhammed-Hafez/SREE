About Us

SREE is a Cairo-based interior design studio dedicated to creating spaces that are as meaningful as they are beautiful. We collaborate closely with our clients to translate their vision into interiors that reflect their personality, lifestyle, and aspirations — across residential apartments, luxury villas, and bespoke private homes.

Every project we undertake is approached with meticulous attention to detail — from the initial brief and spatial planning through material selection, custom furniture design, and final styling. Our portfolio spans a diverse range of aesthetics, from refined neo-classic elegance to clean contemporary modernism, always rooted in quality craftsmanship and enduring design.
